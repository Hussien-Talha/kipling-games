How it works A note from the maker

From the paper to your phone

  1. GAMES

    Open the games page

    Every issue of the school newspaper has a games page, and every issue brings a new game.

  2. SCAN

    Scan the code

    Point your phone's camera at the QR code. The game opens in your browser, with no app and no sign-up.

  3. 456

    Pick a length

    Choose 4, 5 or 6 letters. Each length keeps its own streak, so you can get good at all three.

  4. GUESS

    Six guesses

    Type a real word and press Enter. Orange is the right letter in the right place, blue is the right letter in the wrong place.

  5. AGAIN

    Then another

    There's no one-a-day limit. Finish a word, tap Next word and keep going for as long as you like.

  6. NEXT

    A new game next issue

    The next paper brings a different game to this same page, so the QR code keeps working.

A note from the maker

I made Just So Words for the games page of our school newspaper, so you can carry a puzzle home in your pocket.

"Just so" means exactly right, which is what a tile turns when you find a letter's place.

The word lists hold about 2,900 everyday words. I went through them so that names and anything unsuitable for school are left out. If a word seems wrong, or one is missing, tell the newspaper team and I'll fix it.

Your scores stay on your own phone. The game doesn't ask for your name and doesn't collect anything.

Hussien Talha
