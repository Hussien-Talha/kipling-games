Can you guess the word?

Guess the Word is this month's game from the Kipling Chronicles: find the hidden word in six tries, then play another.

How many letters?

1,200 five-letter words to find

How it works

From the paper to your phone

  1. GAMES

    Open the games page

    Every issue of the Kipling Chronicles has a games page, and every issue brings a new game.

  2. SCAN

    Scan the code

    Point your phone's camera at the QR code. The game opens in your browser, with no app and no sign-up.

  3. 456

    Pick a length

    Choose 4, 5 or 6 letters. Each length keeps its own streak, so you can get good at all three.

  4. GUESS

    Six guesses

    Type a real word and press Enter. Orange is the right letter in the right place, blue is the right letter in the wrong place.

  5. AGAIN

    Then another

    There's no one-a-day limit. Finish a word, tap Next word and keep going for as long as you like.

  6. NEXT

    A new game next issue

    The next paper brings a different game to this same page, so the QR code keeps working.

See you next issue

Keep the QR code from this month's Kipling Chronicles. It always opens this page, and this page always has the newest game.
