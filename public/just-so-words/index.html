Made by Hussien Talha · Kipling School Games

How to play

Find the hidden word in six guesses. Each guess must be a real word. Press Enter to check it.

After each guess, the tiles change colour:

PLANT

Orange: P is in the word, in the right place.

STORY

Blue: T is in the word, but in a different place.

CHAIR

Grey: A is not in the word at all.

Pick 4, 5 or 6 letters at the top. When you finish a word, tap Next word to play another. There's no limit.

Each word length keeps its own streak. Your progress is saved on this phone only.
